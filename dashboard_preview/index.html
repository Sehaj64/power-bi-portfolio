🚨 Stop the Profit Leakage on Tables
Our analysis indicates that while the **Furniture** category generates strong sales volumes, the **Tables** sub-category is heavily unprofitable. This is caused by excessive discount margins in states like Texas and Illinois (regularly exceeding 40%). We recommend capping standard table discounts to a maximum of 15%.
🚀 Shift Marketing Focus to Corporate Tech
**Technology** products (specifically Phones and Copiers) represent the highest net margins in our catalog. Marketing budgets should be dynamically shifted to target corporate segment buyers in high-profitability hubs like New York and California.
